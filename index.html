Shipping policy

Dispatch

Orders are packed in the order they were placed and leave the unit within five working days of a drop closing. You get one tracking mail when the parcel moves.

Rates

United Kingdom, tracked 48: £4.50, free over £150. Europe: £12. Rest of world: £22. Duties are not included and are the buyer's responsibility.

Returns

Fourteen days from delivery, unworn, tags on. Drop pieces are made once, so an exchange depends on what came back. Refunds go to the original card.

Terms of service

Orders

An order is an offer to buy. It is accepted when the parcel is dispatched. Stock is counted at the moment of payment; oversold items are refunded in full within 24 hours.

One per person

Drops are limited to one of each piece per household. Orders that look like resale stacking are cancelled and refunded, and the address is not served again.

Demonstration

This storefront is a build, not a business. Every product, price and policy below is invented, no payment is taken, and no email is collected.
